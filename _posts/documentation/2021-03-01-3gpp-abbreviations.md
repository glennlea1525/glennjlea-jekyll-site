# {{ page.title }}

I spent some years learning and writing about mobile technology including learning about various wireless spectrums and 2G/3G/4G mobile technologies. These technology allow you to make phone calls on mobile devices, download apps, send messages, check bank accounts and indulge in social media.

The technology used to send signals to your phone from mobile towers are essentially analog signals. Once those signals hit the tower, they are converted to digital form which the mobile phone uses.
