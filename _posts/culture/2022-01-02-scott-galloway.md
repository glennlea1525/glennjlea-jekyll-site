# {{ page.title }}

I'm a big fan of the **Pivot podcast** hosted by Recode’s Kara Swisher and NYU Professor Scott Galloway. Kara Swisher is the Editor-at-Large at New York Magazine and founder of Recode. Scott Galloway is a Marketing Professor at New York University (NYU).

In this video, Scott Galloway speaks at the 2021 Code Conference and provides a powerful critique of the underhyped and overhyped trends in business. It is well worth viewing. He speaks about the impact of the Pandemic on business in the United States and around the world, especially its imbalances and missed opportunities. His last point is about the most undervalued opportunity available today.
