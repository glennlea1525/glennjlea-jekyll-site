The Haudenosaunee
Confederacy

The Haudenosaunee are a confederation of six Iroquois-speaking First Nations peoples that lived south of the Lake Ontario - the Seneca, Cayuga, Oneida, Onondaga Mohawk and the Tuscarora nation. How they developed into a confederation is an amazing story and worth documenting.

John Ware, Canada's Legendary Cowboy (1845-1905)

John Ware was a former slave who became a legendary cowboy in the latter decades of the nineteen century Alberta, Canada. Highly respected amongst his peers, he was unique amongst the tough cowboys of Alberta. His story is a reflection of what Canada aspires to be in the twenty-first century.
